Why AI budgets leak

Tool sprawl

Multiple teams buy overlapping AI tools with little central visibility or ownership.

Wrong model selection

Premium models used for low-complexity tasks that cheaper models handle just as well.

Repeated context

The same documents and background uploaded again and again, paying to reprocess identical content.

No evaluation

Model choice based on reputation and habit, not evidence from your actual workflows.

No value visibility

Leaders see subscription spend but cannot connect it to business outcomes.

Governance gaps

Teams experiment with sensitive data without clear permissions or accountability.
