The gap

SME legal work is too small to bill and too risky to ignore.

  • Firms turn it away or underprice it. Probation questions, leave balances and gratuity sums take a senior lawyer's time and pay little at hourly rates.
  • SMEs guess, or ask a general chatbot. General tools answer UAE questions with the wrong jurisdiction, invented numbers and no one accountable.
  • The risky cases look routine at first. "Can I dismiss him?" is a dismissal decision. A good system knows when to stop answering.
How it works

Four steps, one accountable lawyer.

01

The SME asks

In Arabic or English, after stating where the business is licensed and who is asking.

02

Triage runs first

Court, police, dispute, drafting, high-stakes and out-of-scope signals are checked before any answer.

03

Approved cards answer

Low-risk questions get general information from the firm's article-referenced knowledge cards. Nothing else.

04

The lawyer decides

Everything else lands in a review console: approve, amend with a note, or book a call.
